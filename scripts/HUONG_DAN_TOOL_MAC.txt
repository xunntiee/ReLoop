POST TO VIDEO - HUONG DAN CHO macOS
==================================

BAT DAU NHANH

1. Tai dung file theo may:
   - macOS arm64 / Apple Silicon: MacBook chip M1, M2, M3, M4...
   - macOS x64 / Intel: MacBook chip Intel.
2. Giai nen file ZIP ra mot thu muc tren may. Khong chay tool ngay trong file ZIP.
3. May can co Google Chrome hoac Microsoft Edge. Tool uu tien Chrome, sau do tu dong thu Edge.
4. Nhan dup "OPEN TOOL.command". Doi 5-30 giay, giao dien se mo trong trinh duyet.
5. Khi khong dung nua, nhan dup "STOP TOOL.command" de luu tien do va tat cac tien trinh nen.

LAN CHAY DAU BI macOS CHAN

- Nhan chuot phai vao "OPEN TOOL.command", chon Open, sau do chon Open them mot lan.
- Neu van bi chan, vao System Settings > Privacy & Security va chon Open Anyway.
- Day la xac nhan bao mat mot lan cho goi noi bo chua notarize.

YEU CAU

- macOS 12 Monterey tro len.
- Google Chrome hoac Microsoft Edge.
- Ket noi Internet cho dang nhap Threads, crawl, license va dich vu giong noi.
- Tai khoan Threads da dang nhap, license key hop le va Voice API da cau hinh.
- Khong can cai Node.js, Bun, Playwright, Chromium hay FFmpeg. Cac thanh phan runtime can thiet
  da nam trong goi portable.

DU LIEU VA NHAT KY

- Cau hinh, du lieu va log duoc luu tai:
  ~/Library/Application Support/ToolBase/
- De cap nhat, mo muc Cai dat trong tool va chon "Import ban moi (.zip)". Chon dung
  goi macOS arm64 hoac Intel; tool se tu dong dong, thay ban moi va mo lai.
- Khong can xoa thu muc ban cu va khong mat cau hinh trong thu muc du lieu ca nhan.
- Du lieu ca nhan khong nam trong thu muc tool, vi vay thay ban portable khong lam mat cau hinh.

Neu ban moi khong khoi dong duoc, tool tu khoi phuc ban truoc. Xem log tai:
  ~/Library/Application Support/ToolBase/updates/updater.log

NEU TOOL KHONG MO

1. Kiem tra Chrome/Edge da duoc cai.
2. Chay "STOP TOOL.command", sau do mo lai.
3. Xem log tai:
   ~/Library/Application Support/ToolBase/logs/launcher-error.log
   ~/Library/Application Support/ToolBase/logs/server.err.log
